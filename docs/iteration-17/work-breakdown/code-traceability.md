## Open work and limits

- **SCRUM-257:** the original feedback, exact cleanup release and team-review evidence are unavailable. Current source/navigation checks are separate evidence and do not close that historical contribution requirement.
- The shot rebuild quarantines **14 games / 2,506 shots** with inconsistent scores. **Ten** retained uncertain locations are excluded from spatial outputs. **110,206** ambiguous missed-attempt values remain unknown; geometry-derived zones are marked accordingly.
- Shot rankings estimate **points per attempt**, using season-wide offense and opponent defense. Heatmap shot-type/zone filters apply to the image, not the rankings. The heatmap counts makes and misses, and reports excluded and cap-omitted attempts separately.
- Shared report exports preserve a captured displayed-row snapshot. Specialized shot CSV/PDF exports regenerate from selected inputs and record the loaded source versions. The PNG and saved-metrics CSV routes additionally reject a changed expected version. These are different contracts.
- Saved shot results require proposed-shot location/type. **BST-117 remains Fail**, **SCRUM-641 remains planned**, and the broader **BST-114 suite remains in progress**. Neither these diagnostics nor regular retrospective calibration proves pre-possession forecast accuracy.
- The recorded coach-baseline timings meet their agreed limits. The three Quick Call browser timings are ordinary observations, not a two-second service guarantee.
