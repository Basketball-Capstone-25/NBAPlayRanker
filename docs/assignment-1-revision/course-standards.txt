Course requirements

These notes follow the SLATE lessons. The course PDFs stay outside the shared
repository.

Project Planning with JIRA, pages 15-16 and 26-28
Use clear task titles, an assignee and acceptance criteria. Each Dev Subtask
needs an Effort Estimate of no more than four hours. Log actual hours separately.

SCRUM-551 through SCRUM-565 break down the interaction, risk and test revisions
under SCRUM-528, SCRUM-529 and SCRUM-530. Their estimates range from 0.5 to 3
hours. See tracking/ for the tasks and acceptance criteria.

Test Planning, pages 8-23
Each test needs a functional area, use case, inputs, steps and expected result.
Record the observed result and link failures to fix tickets. Rerun those tests
after the fixes. Unfinished checks stay Verify.

Page 15 requires Epic-level Test Suites and Task-level Test Cases. The 115
cases and 14 suites now use that structure. Each case is back under its
original suite, with its key, results, links and owner kept. See hierarchy/.

Interaction and Detailed Design, pages 4-16
The five complete use-case collaborations keep their realization links,
overview diagrams and Design Model classifiers. This revision fixes sequence
lifelines, parameters and replies. The interaction guide lists the diagram
locations and model-organization work still needed for later features.

Before submission
Combine these changes with the other group member's architecture, deployment
and timeline revisions. Include the native model and shared Jira and Visual
Paradigm links. Confirm the deadline and professor access. No SLATE upload
has been made.
