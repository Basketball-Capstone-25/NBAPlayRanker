What the numbers mean
- Observed PPP = recorded offensive points / recorded offensive possessions.
- Modeled PPP = sum(predicted PPP x selected play possessions) / selected possessions.
- Difference = modeled PPP - observed PPP.
- Play usage = play possessions / all recorded offensive season possessions x 100.

Limits and remaining checks
These summaries use regular-season play-type totals. They do not provide
last-five/last-ten-game results. Modeled differences are historical comparisons,
not measured improvement or future predictions. Usage covers recorded play-type
possessions, not independently verified whole-game possessions. The clutch
report uses a separate shot dataset and its own selected situation filters.
