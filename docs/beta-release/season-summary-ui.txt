Season summary screens — SCRUM-776

What changed
The coach Matchup and analyst Data Explorer now show the season summary inline.
It compares observed team PPP with the modeled Top-K average and shows each
selected play type's share of the full offensive season.

The coach summary follows the rankings from Run Baseline, including normalized
weights and Top-K. Changing inputs clears it until the next run finishes.
The analyst summary uses the selected season and offensive team, a separate
opponent, Top 5 and 70/30 weights. Table-only filters do not change its totals.
Defense, all-teams and shot-data views do not show an offensive play-type summary.

How to explain the code
app/domain/season-summary.ts checks the response matches the requested filters
and that usage totals and PPP subtraction agree before displaying the numbers.
app/infrastructure/season-summary.ts sends the session token in the header and
handles errors, timeout, cancellation and retry. An old reply cannot replace
the current selection.
app/ui/_shared/components/SeasonSummary.tsx displays the values and usage bars.
The coach page supplies its completed ranking filters. AnalystSeasonSummary.tsx
adds the separate opponent selector and limits the panel to one offensive team.
