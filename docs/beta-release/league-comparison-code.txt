League comparison — code guide (SCRUM-697)

This section adds a coach view of team rates beside the league average.
It uses one team, one regular season and either offense or defense.
The links below point to build a3a321b66ace9ad3da0a134f953e258ec7e53e7b.

1. Where the rates come from
backend/domain/baseline_recommendation/baseline_recommender.py, lines 68–78
https://github.com/Basketball-Capstone-25/NBAPlayRanker/blob/a3a321b66ace9ad3da0a134f953e258ec7e53e7b/backend/domain/baseline_recommendation/baseline_recommender.py#L68-L78
The new view reuses these existing calculations. FG% is total field goals made
divided by total attempts. eFG% is sum(source eFG rate × attempts) / total attempts.
Turnover and free-throw possession rates use sum(source rate × possessions)
divided by total possessions. They are not averages of the displayed percentages.

2. How the team is compared with the league
backend/application/analytics_services/league_comparison.py, lines 49–73
https://github.com/Basketball-Capstone-25/NBAPlayRanker/blob/a3a321b66ace9ad3da0a134f953e258ec7e53e7b/backend/application/analytics_services/league_comparison.py#L49-L73
Both sides use the same season, side and play type. The league includes the
selected team. Difference = 100 × (team rate − league rate), in percentage points.
If a category or denominator is missing, its rate and difference stay unavailable.

4. What the table shows
app/ui/recommendation/_components/LeagueComparisonTable.tsx, lines 30–53
https://github.com/Basketball-Capstone-25/NBAPlayRanker/blob/a3a321b66ace9ad3da0a134f953e258ec7e53e7b/app/ui/recommendation/_components/LeagueComparisonTable.tsx#L30-L53
Each play type has a team row, league row and difference row. Counts show the
sample behind the rates. A sample warning appears when possessions or attempts
fall below the selected cutoff. Changing the cutoff never changes the rates.
The calculation details explain the source rounding and missing values.

5. What happens when a selection changes or a request fails
app/ui/recommendation/_components/LeagueComparisonPanel.tsx, lines 24–42
https://github.com/Basketball-Capstone-25/NBAPlayRanker/blob/a3a321b66ace9ad3da0a134f953e258ec7e53e7b/app/ui/recommendation/_components/LeagueComparisonPanel.tsx#L24-L42
A new team, season or side gets fresh loading state. Old replies cannot replace
the current selection. A failed request shows a reason and Retry comparison.

Example to explain: TOR, 2024–25, Offense, Cut
TOR has 381 made field goals from 619 attempts, across 745 possessions.
TOR FG% = 381 / 619 × 100 = 61.5509%, displayed as 61.6%.
League FG% = 66.7718%, displayed as 66.8%.
Difference = 61.5509 − 66.7718 = −5.2209 percentage points, displayed as −5.2 pp.
The calculation uses full precision before rounding the display.
The league sample has 14,274 attempts and 17,011 possessions.

To show it, sign in as a coach and open Matchup dashboard → League comparison.
Select TOR, 2024–25 and Offense. Find Cut and open How these rates are calculated.
Change the sample cutoff to 1,000: warnings change but the rates stay the same.
Switch to Defense to see rates allowed by that defense. Higher is not always better.
These are historical profiles, not forecasts or direct games against one opponent.
Free-throw possession rate is not free-throw shooting accuracy. The possession
rates can overlap; eFG%, turnover and free-throw rates use rounded source rates.
