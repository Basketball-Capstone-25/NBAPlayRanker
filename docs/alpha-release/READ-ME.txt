Gurk's two new use cases

SCRUM-695 — Compare Usage and Efficiency
Compare how often a team uses each play type and its points per possession
with the league for that same play type. The chart and table use the same
figures. Possession counts and small-sample labels help explain the results.

SCRUM-696 — View Opponent Defensive Weak Spots
Compare the opponent's allowed points per possession with the league. The
view highlights up to three above-average areas that meet the selected
possession cutoff. Unavailable play types are shown as missing, not zero.

Both views describe regular-season history. They do not predict the next game
or show that running a play more often will improve the team's performance.
